About Us

Welcome to Instagram Video Downloader. We provide information and a simple interface for users interested in saving publicly accessible Instagram videos when downloading is permitted.

Our purpose is to offer a straightforward, mobile-friendly experience with clear information about supported content, service limitations and responsible use.

Who Can Use This Website?

Our website is intended for people who want to save their own content or content they have permission to download. Users should always respect the rights and privacy of content creators.

How the Service Works

The website provides an interface where users can submit a supported public Instagram post or Reel link. Availability depends on whether the content can be accessed and downloaded.

Private posts, login-restricted content and content protected by access controls are not supported. We do not guarantee that every submitted link will work or that the service will always be available.
